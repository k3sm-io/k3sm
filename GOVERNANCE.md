# Governance

> **Status: bootstrapping.** k3sm is in early development with a small founding
> maintainer set. This document describes the **intended** governance model the
> project is growing into (a maintainer-council model); the voting
> thresholds below become operative as the maintainer count grows.
> [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) reflects the current reality.

## Roles

- **Contributor** — anyone who submits a contribution (code, docs, issues,
  reviews). No prior status required beyond the [DCO](DCO) sign-off.
- **Reviewer** — a contributor with a track record who reviews PRs in an area.
  Reviews are advisory until a maintainer approves.
- **Maintainer** — holds merge rights and release responsibility, listed in
  [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Maintainers are added by the existing
  maintainers based on sustained, high-quality contribution.

## Decision making

- **Routine changes** proceed by **lazy consensus**: a PR with at least one
  maintainer approval and no unresolved objections may merge.
- **Substantial or architectural changes** use the project's structured
  design-review process (a multi-perspective critique before the change lands);
  significant decisions are recorded in the design docs.
- **Disagreements** are resolved by discussion; if consensus cannot be reached,
  a simple majority of maintainers decides.

## Changes to governance and the maintainer set

Adding or removing a maintainer, or changing this document, requires a
**supermajority (two-thirds)** of the current maintainers. (While the project has
a single founding maintainer, this threshold is necessarily met by that
maintainer; it becomes meaningful as the council grows.)

## Code of Conduct

All participation is governed by the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
